The Layers panel keeps asking until the picture has something to show: a slow machine that answered before the film's first frame was drawn used to leave the panel empty until the next seek or edit. A clip that says it has its own sound is marked in the timeline (`data-own-audio`), so the clip menu's Mute and the panel can be waited for.

A selected layer whose page is drawn anew (a paste undone, say) is kept for six seconds instead of one: on a slow machine the page took longer to come back and the selection was dropped, so the layers a person still had in hand were lost.
